# เฮราคลีส: เควสไถ่บาปที่นับไม่ครบ — Treatment

Style: HD-2D (styles/hd-2d/STYLE.md) · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · ~2:38 · Thai narration (user's recording) + burned-in subs + .srt
Episode 2 of the "ตำนานเทพ MMORPG" series (after Perseus). Same UI language (gold-on-dark plates, Taviraj/Trirong).

## Three structures considered
1. Chronological labour list (12 short beats) — reads as a list, no fight has room to breathe.
2. **Cold open mid-fight with the Hydra, rewind, three full action fights + a fast quest-log montage** (chosen):
   the user asked for continuous hack-and-slash combat; three fights each with a different "trick" (invulnerable → grab;
   regrowing heads → party combo; no-weapons rule → bare-hand boss with three HP bars) keep each fight distinct.
3. Overworld map hub returning between labours — too static for an action episode.

## Logline / arc
A demigod with a goddess as his personal nemesis grinds twelve penance quests for a coward king who hides in a jar —
and the final twist: the nemesis becomes his mother-in-law.

## Benchmark (grammar only)
Real-time action RPGs: hit-stop, screen shake, slash trails, floating damage numbers, combo counter, skill-name banners,
slow-motion finisher. MMO top-down camera over a diorama. No UI, names, skills or designs of any existing game.

## Combat grammar (new for this episode)
- Fights are continuous (no command menu). Every hit: 60–90 ms hit-stop, shake, impact spark, white flash on the target, knockback.
- Floating numbers: grey "0"/MISS on invulnerable hits, white numbers, gold CRITICAL. Combo counter "N HITS" top right.
- Skill banner over the hero's head when a named move fires ("ล็อกคอ!", "ฟัน+จี้ไฟ!").
- Finisher: 0.6 s slow-motion + camera dive + big hit + silence beat, then the KO.
- Fights carry no narration (lion 44.0–49.6, hydra 64.7–68.2 and 73.3–79.6, Cerberus 124.7–132.4): picture and SFX tell it.

choir for Olympus. Foley: sword clangs (lion, metal-on-hide), meaty hits, roars, hiss, fire whoosh, rumble, jar lid clank.
